# Curriculum review list

For the native French speaker and the pastor / mission leader who review the curriculum simplification
([curriculum-simplification-plan.md](curriculum-simplification-plan.md)). Nothing here is final until they have read it.
The shortened forms are **new phrases**: the long originals keep their text and keys and moved later in the course.

## 1. New phrases (20)

Please check the French, the English gloss, the note and the `tu` form. All main phrases are in the vous register; `tu` is
given only where the check script accepts it (imperatives have none).

| # | Lesson | French | English | Note | tu |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | u0-l3 | Monsieur | Sir | Said 'muh-syuh'; the r is silent. It is the polite word for addressing a man. |  |
| 5 | u1-l2 | Et vous ? | And you? | Hand the question back after you answer: Très bien, merci. Et vous ? | Et toi ? |
| 12 | u13-l3 | Bienvenue ! | Welcome! | Bien (well) plus venue (come). Say it when someone arrives at your church or event. |  |
| 13 | u12-l1 | Nous sommes étudiants | We are students | Use étudiantes if the whole group is women. The final s is silent. |  |
| 17 | u5-l3 | Il est deux heures | It's two o'clock | Add heures (hours) after the number. For one o'clock it is il est une heure. |  |
| 18 | u5-l4 | Lundi | Monday | Day names have no capital letter in the middle of a sentence. |  |
| 18 | u5-l4 | Samedi | Saturday | Said 'sam-dee'. |  |
| 18 | u5-l4 | Dimanche | Sunday | Said 'dee-mahnsh'; the an is a nasal sound. |  |
| 20 | u5-l6 | Aujourd'hui | Today | One word with an apostrophe in the middle. Said 'oh-zhoor-dwee'. |  |
| 22 | u2-l2 | Tournez à droite | Turn right | Tournez means turn. You will mostly hear this one rather than say it. |  |
| 23 | u2-l3 | Un aller simple, s'il vous plaît | A one-way ticket, please | Aller simple is one way. Say it at the ticket desk. |  |

Questions for the native speaker:

* Is *Monsieur* on its own natural as "Sir" (lesson 3), and *Et vous ?* as a follow-up to *Très bien, merci* (lesson 5)?
* Is *Pas de viande, s'il vous plaît* natural to a waiter? Is *C'est à cinq minutes* natural for "it's five minutes away"
  (the long form *C'est à cinq minutes à pied* is in u20-l2)?
* *Un aller simple, s'il vous plaît* and *Un aller-retour, s'il vous plaît* at a ticket desk; *Le plat du jour*, *Une tarte
  aux pommes*, *Un dessert, s'il vous plaît* as ordering lines.
* *Bienvenue !* (lesson 12) as what a church member says to a visitor.
* **Added by the owner's decision 3, not in the original plan:** *Joyeux Noël !* and *Un chocolat chaud, s'il vous plaît* in
  the optional Christmas-market unit u21 (the unit needed five phrases once the three market-only lines left u10-l2).
* Whether the reordered early lessons (3 to 7, 12 to 14) read naturally as small dialogues.

## 3. Owner decisions built in

1. T0 load up to 7 (mean 6.4) and T1 up to 9 (mean 7.7), no cut to 5 phrases per lesson.
2. *Je peux vous parler de ma foi ?* stays in u12-l2 (lesson 14), next to *Puis-je prier pour vous ?*, as a fixed formula.
   `scripts/check-curriculum.mjs` lists it as a formulaic chunk (one slot, one unit), so it is exempt from the length cap.
   Pastor: is lesson 14 too early for this question?
3. The market-only lines (*Où est le marché de Noël ?*, *Des bredele, s'il vous plaît*, *Je rends la tasse pour récupérer la
   consigne*) are in **u21 "Marché de Noël (optional)"**, the last unit. It is seasonal and not needed for the March trip.
   *Un jus de pomme chaud* and the food lines stay in u10-l2, now titled "Alsatian food".
4. Faith words first: u13 (God, Jesus, the Bible, prayer, *Vous êtes invité*) is lessons 10 to 12, before numbers.
   Pastor: *Dieu vous aime* (lesson 13) and *Puis-je prier pour vous ?* (lesson 14) come before learners can do much
   small talk; is the note "once there is some warmth" enough? Testimony (*Jésus a changé ma vie*) is lesson 39.
5. No allergy phrases were added (out of scope). *Je suis allergique aux cacahuètes* stays in u3-l4 (lesson 28).
