How to read this: screen 2 is the product. In the real app "Show them (full screen)" hides all app chrome and makes the circles huge so you can hold up your phone, like Translate's "Show them". The step captions are what you say out loud; the Words tab teaches those same sentences in French, with the phone's French voice. Everything on screens 2 to 4 can be edited by an admin (screen 4), in both languages.
